Our Story

15 years of building homes.
One too many missed bids.

Spencer Stephens started Spencer Stephens Construction in 2009 with a clear principle: homeowners deserve a builder who communicates clearly, plans thoroughly, and takes genuine pride in their work.

Over 100 homes later, SSC Homes has earned an A+ BBB rating, membership in the Northern Wasatch Home Builders Association, and a reputation for craftsmanship across Northern Utah.

But every single estimate was a grind. Three to five days of chasing sub quotes. Inconsistent pricing. Bids delivered slow. Some clients didn't wait around.

SSC Estimating was built to fix that. Not as a side hustle — as a genuine tool we use ourselves, productized so other GCs and subs can benefit from the same speed.
