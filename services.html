Core Feature 01

Instant Cost Engine

Enter your project details and get a full trade-by-trade cost breakdown in under 20 minutes. No spreadsheets. No sub phone calls. No waiting.

The cost engine is powered by a continuously updated database of real Northern Utah project costs. It accounts for square footage, project type, finish level, site conditions, and seasonal material fluctuations.

  • ✓ Trade-by-trade itemization (framing, foundation, MEP, finishing)
  • ✓ Material and labor separated by line item
  • ✓ Confidence score per estimate based on data density
  • ✓ Adjustable contingency and overhead markup

Core Feature 02

Sub-Rate Database

Stop calling six subs to find out what framing costs this month. Our database tracks active sub rates across all major trades in Northern Utah — updated continuously.

Each rate entry is timestamped, sourced from real projects, and averaged across multiple subs to give you a defensible market price — not a guess.

  • ✓ Framing, foundation, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, roofing, finishing
  • ✓ Rate per sq ft, per unit, and per linear foot where applicable
  • ✓ Sub ratings and historical reliability tracking
  • ✓ Override any rate with your own preferred sub's number
